Answering Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick clarifications about site estimates, material selections, and layout planning.

Do you manufacture modular cabinets in-house?

Yes. All modular cabinets, wardrobes, and TV backdrops are framed and fabricated by our expert carpenters under strict quality guidelines before on-site assembly.

Which plywood grade do you specify for bathroom vanities?

We specify high-durability BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Grade Plywood exclusively for humid zones like toilets, kitchens, and vanity cabinets to ensure zero wood swelling.

What districts in Kerala do you serve?

We actively manage residential and commercial interior design contracting projects throughout Palakkad, Thrissur, and Malappuram districts.
