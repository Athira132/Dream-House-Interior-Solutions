Bathroom & Vanity Design

Bespoke vanity cabinets utilizing Boiling Water Proof (BWP) plywood, backlit accent mirrors, and layout configurations that resist moisture.

Moisture Engineering

Elegance in Damp Zones

Bathrooms require specialized materials. We build vanities utilizing BWP Plywood which prevents wood swelling or warping due to constant contact with humidity.

From counter-mounted stone basins to floating cabinetry with acrylic facades, we configure layout placements that maximize your toilet floor space.

"A vanity counter coordinates wash utility with clean lines, hiding pipe routes within moisture-proof casing panels."
