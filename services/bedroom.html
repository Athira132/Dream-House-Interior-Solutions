FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about customized bedrooms and sliding wardrobes.

Do you build custom cot frameworks?

Yes. We fabricate custom beds on-site featuring integrated hydraulic lift storage compartments and soft-padded headboards.

What wardrobe door configurations are available?

We install sliding mirror panels, space-saving folding profiles, and classic soft-closing hinged swing doors depending on room size.

How are dressing mirror lights integrated?

We build back-lit dressing frameworks using sandblasted glass panes embedded with warm LED strips for uniform lighting.
