FAQ

Common Queries

Quick clarifications about site estimates and engineering checks.

Do you manage civil contracting?

Yes. We coordinate concrete floor layouts, partition walls, and plumbing channels alongside our custom carpentry to maintain structural consistency.

What is the execution schedule?

Projects are staged over bi-weekly milestones. Detailed on-site photographs and checklist reports are sent to clients regularly.

How are material quantities estimated?

We issue detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) sheets showing lengths, unit rates, and brands before site operations commence.
