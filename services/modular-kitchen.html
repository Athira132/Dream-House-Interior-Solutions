FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about custom modular kitchens and hardware.

What wood grade do you use for wet zones?

We use premium 18mm BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Plywood exclusively for kitchen sink zones to prevent swelling and rot.

Do you install gas lines?

Yes. Concealed copper piping and venting channels are routed cleanly during structural plumbing preparation.

What kitchen accessories are included?

Our standard layout packages include silverware trays, soft-close drawers, corner carousel shelves, plate racks, and spice pullouts.
