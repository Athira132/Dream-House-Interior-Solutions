Modular Kitchen Design

Modern kitchens combining efficient planning triangle guidelines, smart storage pullout accessories, and premium moisture-resistant finishes.

Functional Ergonomics

The Efficiency Triangle

A kitchen should support speed and ease of movement. We plan layouts based on the working triangle principles, placing the range, sink, and refrigerator in configurations that prevent unnecessary steps.

We utilize high-durability Marine Plywood (BWR Grade) to resist water damage and humidity, wrapping shutters in acrylic, laminates, or lacquer finishes.

"A well-planned kitchen blends spatial movement with durable structures that stand up to daily use."
