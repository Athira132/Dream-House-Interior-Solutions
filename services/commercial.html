FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about corporate partition layouts and desk channels.

Do you construct soundproof partitions?

Yes. We erect double-layer gypsum partitions stuffed with high-density rockwool sheets to limit noise leaks between cabins.

How are desk network cables managed?

We build metal cable trays beneath desktop surfaces and set flush floor trunking blocks to keep wires organized.

Can you coordinate layouts to match fire norms?

Yes. We use fire-rated board panels and keep paths wide to align with local commercial safety codes.
