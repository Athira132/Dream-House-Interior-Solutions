FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about spatial models and layout drafting.

What is included in the 3D rendering package?

Comprehensive furniture placement blueprints, false ceiling POP shapes, texture coatings, spotlights, and matching fabric highlights are represented realistically.

How many revisions are allowed?

We provide three rounds of layout revisions, adjusting wall lines, cabinet shelves, and texture codes based on your site feedback.

Do you provide blueprint files for local sub-contractors?

Yes. Final CAD mapping files (.dwg) and dimension sheets are provided to ensure your local technicians execute elevations correctly.
