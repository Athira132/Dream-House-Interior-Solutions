FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about custom glass partitions and safety.

What is the standard glass thickness specified?

We install 10mm or 12mm thick toughened safety glass (Saint-Gobain brand) with polished pencil edges for maximum strength.

How are partition gaps soundproofed?

We pack EPDM rubber gaskets into metal track joints and inject acoustic silicone seals to reduce voice transfer.

Can you apply custom frosted designs?

Yes. Custom geometric lines, privacy frost levels, or your corporate logo films can be pasted on-site.
