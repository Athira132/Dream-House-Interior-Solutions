FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about custom false ceilings and joint mesh.

What ensures ceiling joint stability?

We use fiberglass joint mesh tapes and high-bonding joint compounds to seal board seams, preventing hairline plaster cracks.

How deep are light coves planned?

Our standard LED cove channels are recessed 100mm to 150mm to allow soft light dispersal without spots.

Does false ceiling reduce room heat?

Yes. The dead air gap between the structural concrete slab and gypsum ceiling panels provides excellent acoustic and thermal insulation.
