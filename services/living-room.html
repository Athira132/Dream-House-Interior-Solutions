FAQ

Common Queries

Quick questions about custom living consoles and wall finishes.

What is the standard TV unit backing material?

We use premium 18mm BWR (Boiling Water Resistant) marine plywood coated in veneer or acrylic to prevent moisture warping and ensure structural stability.

Do you install acoustic paneling?

Yes. Sound-absorbing wooden slatted panels can be integrated directly behind the television backdrop to optimize audio clarity.

How is wiring concealed?

We route fire-resistant PVC conduits behind the wall paneling structure, keeping cords, adaptors, and gaming console wires hidden.
