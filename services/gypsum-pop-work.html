Architectural Ceilings

Sculpted Light Overlays

A plain concrete slab restricts lighting layouts. We construct Gypsum and Plaster of Paris (POP) false ceilings to run hidden electrical wires and drop spotlights exactly where needed.

From minimalist flat ceilings with magnetic track tracks to complex multi-step coves, our plasterers execute smooth transitions and sharp corners.

"A false ceiling frames a room, balancing height perceptions through recessed light profiles and neat cornice junctions."

Steps

How We Create It

Meticulous leveling checks to prevent ceiling waves or cracks over time.

01

Water Level Mapping

Marking reference heights across walls physically to guarantee a level grid frame.

02

GI Metal Channel Framing

Securing galvanized iron channels to ceilings with fasteners to support gypsum weights.

03

Gypsum Board Patching

Screwing gypsum sheets to GI channels, cutting spotlight circles, and routing wires.

04

Tape Jointing & Putty

Applying fiber joint tapes, double-coating joint compound putty, and sanding flat before paint.
